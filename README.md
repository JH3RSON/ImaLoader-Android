> [!TIP]
> ### 🎵 A Message for Music Lovers
> Right now, **ImaLoader** outputs audio in **standard AAC format at 128 kbps**. While this is optimal for fast video previews and voice clips, I know many of you want to save music tracks and songs with rich, studio-grade clarity.
>
> **Here is what I am actively engineering right now:**
> 
> - 🎼 **Higher Audio Bitrates:** Expanding the internal engine to extract audio at maximum quality (up to **256 kbps / 320 kbps** wherever original sources allow), so your music sounds full, crisp, and punchy on any speaker or headphones.
> - ⚡ **Lossless Stream Demuxing:** Separating audio directly without re-encoding to avoid quality loss.
> - 🏷️ **Smart Music Metadata (Coming Soon):** Working toward automatic fetching of track titles, artist details, and album covers directly into the saved file.
> 
> *Major app releases packed with these improvements are already on the way!*
