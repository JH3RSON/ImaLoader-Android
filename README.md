## ✨ Key Features & Capabilities

ImaLoader connects directly to media endpoints so you get raw, uncompressed files straight to your phone:

- 🎬 **Maximum Native Video Quality:** Downloads streams at their absolute highest available resolution (up to 4K / 60 FPS) without third-party compression or artifacts.
- 🎵 **Reliable Audio Extraction:** Direct extraction of clean audio streams currently formatted in lightweight `AAC 128 kbps`.
- ⚡ **Pure Native Android:** Built from scratch in native Kotlin for lightning-fast launch times and battery-friendly background task handling.
- 📂 **Direct-to-Device Storage:** Media is saved straight to your local device directories and gallery for instant offline access and playback.
- 🛡️ **Zero Tracking, 100% Ad-Free:** No analytics, no account requirements, and no invasive banners. Completely free and non-profit.

> ImaLoader currently downloads audio in standard **AAC 128 kbps**, which is fast and lightweight. However, I am actively reworking the audio pipeline to elevate your listening experience:
